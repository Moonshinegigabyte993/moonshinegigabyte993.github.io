💳 Understanding Lovable Credits

Credits are the currency you spend inside Lovable. Every time you build a page, upload an image, or ask the AI to alter existing code, it consumes a certain number of credits. Many users burn through their initial credit allocation without fully understanding the economy behind it.

In our guide, you'll locate a straightforward table that explains which actions consume credits and at what rate. For example, simple code edits typically cost fewer credits than generating a brand new multi-page application. We also explain the difference between your included monthly credits and promotional ones. It's all laid out in plain language, because the official documentation doesn't always make sense to everyday users.

🎁 Daily Grants and How They Work

One of the most beloved — and overlooked — features of Lovable is the daily credit grant. Instead of forcing you to wait one month, Lovable gives you a small refresh of credits every single day. This is great news if you like to build consistently. But it's also easy to misunderstand how the grant timer works.

Our guide explains when the daily grant resets, whether it accumulates if you miss days, and how you can best use it. Many free users rely on this daily boost to prototype their ideas without spending money. We show you how to check your remaining grant and how to keep track of it without checking the dashboard obsessively. This one section alone can save you from running out at the worst possible moment.

⏳ Credit Expiry: Use Them or Lose Them

Nobody likes reading the expiry policy — until they lose a pile credits. Different packages have different expiry windows. Some credits last seven days, others last thirty, and promotional ones might expire even faster. If you load credits but don't use them in time, they vanish permanently. The guide clearly breaks down:

  • How long each credit type lasts
  • How to track expiry dates before they pass
  • What to do when you have leftover credits near the expiry
  • Whether unused daily grants carry over (in most cases, they don't)

After reading this section, you'll never blindly purchase credits again — you'll know exactly what you're buying and when to use it.

🚫 Cancellation Policy - What Happens Next?

Many users are hesitant to cancel because they fear losing their projects. In reality, Lovable's cancellation process is quite simple, but its aftermath is surprising. When you cancel your paid plan, you effectively lose access to certain premium features, but your previously built apps remain in your account. The guide walks you through the cancellation flow step-by-step, including where to find the cancel button (hidden a few layers deep) and what immediate effects you'll notice.

We also highlight what happens to your daily grants after cancellation: they typically reduce to the free tier number or stop entirely. If you plan to take a break but want to keep your workspace intact, our guide shares a few tips to avoid paying without losing your data. This is essential reading before you even think about pressing cancel.
